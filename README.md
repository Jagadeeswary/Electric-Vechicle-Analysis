⚡ Electric Vehicle Population Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the adoption, distribution, and technological trends of Electric Vehicles (EVs), covering both Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs).

📌 Project Overview

This project explores an Electric Vehicle Population dataset to understand market size, growth patterns, and geographic adoption trends. The goal was to translate raw registration data into a single, decision-ready dashboard that highlights vehicle growth over time, regional adoption, manufacturer/model popularity, and eligibility for clean-fuel incentives.

🧰 Tools & Skills
Power BI Desktop — data modeling, DAX measures, dashboard design
DAX — calculated measures for totals, percentages, and averages
PowerPoint — problem statement & requirements documentation
Data Visualization — KPI cards, line/area charts, choropleth maps, bar charts, donut charts, tree maps

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
KPI	Description
Total Vehicles	Overall count of EVs (BEV + PHEV) in the dataset
Average Electric Range	Mean electric range across all vehicles, indicating tech maturity
Total BEV Vehicles & % of Total	Volume and market share of fully electric vehicles
Total PHEV Vehicles & % of Total	Volume and market share of plug-in hybrid vehicles
📈 Dashboard Visuals
Visual	Chart Type	Purpose
Total Vehicles by Model Year	Line Chart	Adoption growth trend from 2010 onward
Total Vehicles by State	Map (Shape Map)	Geographic distribution and regional hotspots
Top 10 Vehicles by Make	Bar Chart	Manufacturer market dominance
Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility	Donut Chart	Share of vehicles eligible for clean-fuel incentives
Top 10 Vehicles by Model	Tree Map	Most popular EV models by volume
Filters	Slicers	Drill down by City, Electric Vehicle Type, and Electric Utility
